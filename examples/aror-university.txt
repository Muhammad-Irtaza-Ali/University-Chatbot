Aror University — public program context
Source checked: 19 October 2026. This example is not an official handbook or policy.

Aror University of Art, Architecture, Design and Heritage is located in Sukkur, Sindh, Pakistan. The university's main campus is on Rohri Bypass.
Source: https://aror.edu.pk/

The university's Artificial Intelligence program description includes computing, mathematics, statistics, machine learning, artificial neural networks, natural language processing, computer vision, and knowledge representation and reasoning. Its description also discusses ethics, social responsibility, and projects applying AI to real-world domains.
Source: https://newsite.aror.edu.pk/faculty-fest/artificial-intelligence/
